## [1.0.1] - 2026-06-27

### Fixed

- Count device usage days by natural calendar days and include the purchase day.
- Refresh the Widget timeline again after the next local midnight so day counts keep advancing even when the app stays closed.
- Open the edit sheet with the selected device data on the first click instead of requiring a second click.

### Changed

- Rename the summary stats to `当前持有价值` and `当前每日总成本`.
- Limit `当前持有价值` to devices that are still owned.
